A useful loop

Three things, every session. The app does the bookkeeping so you can rest between sets.

1

Log while you train

Spin the dials for weight and reps, tap done. Works offline on the gym floor.

2

See the history

Every exercise keeps its past sessions and a top-set chart with each increase marked.

3

Load the next session

When an increase is due, the next workout opens at the new weight. Change it if the day calls for it.

Load the bar

Start empty and add plates as you need them. Each setup keeps its own data, and none of them touch the private instance at strength.logbook.me.
