Skipping the setting and keeping the threshold fixed at 3 removes that registry edit and that deploy.

The demo's promises ("at least two exercises with an increase marked", "one exercise due today") still hold.
- **Spec-coupled tests (milestone 4).** This is the likeliest place for a red run; the failure messages say what to update.
- **Linked-sets rule (milestone 2).** The one design choice not made by the owner; see the open choice in the draft spec.
