# Daily Multi-Session Exercise Routine (Markdown)

This routine is designed for **Chintan Shah**, age 49, currently on **Zepbound**, with high flexibility in schedule.  
The program spreads activity throughout the day to maximize **fat loss**, **preserve lean mass**, and **improve metabolic health**.

---

# 🏋️ Weekly Structure Overview

- **3 Full Strength Workouts** (Mon/Wed/Fri)
- **2 Zone 2 Cardio Sessions** (Tue/Sat)
- **Multiple Short Exercise Sessions Daily** (“exercise snacks”)
- **1 Optional Conditioning Day** (Thu)
- **1 Rest Day** (Sun)

---

# 📆 Weekly Schedule

## **Monday — Strength Day A + Daily Mini-Sessions**
### **Main Workout (45 minutes)**
1. Goblet Squat — 3 × 10  
2. DB Bench Press — 3 × 8–10  
3. Bent-Over Row — 3 × 10  
4. Romanian Deadlift — 3 × 10  
5. Plank — 3 × 30 sec  

### **Mini Sessions Throughout the Day**
- **Morning:** 10 air squats + 10 incline pushups  
- **Midday:** 5–10 min brisk walk  
- **Afternoon:** 1 min farmer’s carry (DBs) + 30 sec wall sit  
- **After Dinner:** 10 min walk  
- **Evening:** 5 min stretching  

---

## **Tuesday — Zone 2 + Mini Sessions**
### **Zone 2 Cardio (30–40 min)**
- HR target: **115–130 bpm**  
- Fast walking, incline treadmill, cycling, or rowing  

### **Mini Sessions**
- **Morning:** 5 min stair step-ups  
- **Midday:** 5–10 min walk  
- **Afternoon:** 10 lunges per leg  
- **After Dinner:** 10 min walk  

---

## **Wednesday — Strength Day B + Mini Sessions**
### **Main Workout (45 min)**
1. Barbell Deadlift (light/moderate) — 3 × 5  
2. Overhead DB Press — 3 × 8–10  
3. Split Squats — 3 × 8 per leg  
4. Chest-Supported Row — 3 × 10  
5. Dead Bugs — 3 × 6 per side  

### **Mini Sessions**
- **Morning:** 10 squats  
- **Midday:** 10 pushups  
- **Afternoon:** 1 min farmer’s carry  
- **After Dinner:** 10 min walk  

---

## **Thursday — Optional Conditioning + Mini Sessions**
### **Optional Conditioning (10–20 min)**
Choose ONE:  
- Fast walk  
- Light bike ride  
- KB swings  
- DB farmer’s carry intervals  

### **Mini Sessions**
- **Morning:** 10 lunges  
- **Midday:** 5–10 min walk  
- **Afternoon:** 30 sec wall sit  
- **After Dinner:** 10 min walk  

---

## **Friday — Strength Day C + Mini Sessions**
### **Main Workout (45 min)**
1. Barbell Squat — 3 × 6–8  
2. DB Incline Bench — 3 × 8–10  
3. One-Arm DB Row — 3 × 10 per arm  
4. Hip Thrust — 3 × 10  
5. Side Plank — 3 × 20 sec each side  

### **Mini Sessions**
- **Morning:** 10 squats  
- **Midday:** 5–10 min walk  
- **Afternoon:** 1 min step-ups  
- **After Dinner:** 10 min walk  

---

## **Saturday — Zone 2 + Mini Sessions**
### **Zone 2 Cardio (40–45 min)**

### **Mini Sessions**
- **Morning:** 10 incline pushups  
- **Midday:** 10 lunges  
- **Afternoon:** 5 min walk  
- **After Dinner:** 10 min walk  

---

## **Sunday — Rest Day**
Optional light stretching or slow 10-minute walk.

---

# 🎯 Progression Plan
- Add **2.5–5 lbs** weekly to one barbell exercise  
- OR add **1–2 reps** to dumbbell exercises  
- Increase Zone 2 duration by **5 minutes every 2 weeks**

---

# 🧠 Notes for Zepbound Users
- Keep **protein 120–150g/day**  
- Avoid aggressive fasting  
- Strength training is essential to avoid lean mass loss
